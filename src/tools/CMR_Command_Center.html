🛠️ Quick Reference

Answer Status

  • Answered: Patient picked up and spoke with you
  • Voicemail: Call went to voicemail (left message)
  • No Answer: Rang but no answer, no voicemail
  • Busy Signal: Line was busy
  • Wrong Number: Not the patient's number
  • Declined: Patient answered but declined to talk

Outcome

  • CMR Completed: Full medication review finished ✅ (Paid)
  • Rescheduled: Patient wants callback at specific time
  • Refused: Patient not interested in service
  • Callback Later: Patient busy, try again (no specific time)
  • Unable to Reach: After 3 attempts, mark unreachable

🎯 Aspen RxHealth Official QA Coaching Audits & Action Plan

Direct compliance feedback from Aspen QA Evaluators & Pharmacist 100% Pass Standards

🚨 The 10 Mandatory Aspen RxHealth "Zero-Deduction" Golden Rules

1. Disconnected Call-Back Rule:
If call drops and you redial immediately: MUST say "calling on a recorded line" AND re-verify DOB before resuming.
2. Full Address Verification:
Patient must state EVERY element (Street Number, Direction [North/South], Street Type [Ave/Blvd/St], City, State). Prompt if element missed!
3. Full Mailing Address Spoken:
The FULL mailing address must be spoken aloud by either the patient or RPh (RPh can read it back to confirm delivery of MAP).
4. Patient-Stated Health Goals:
Never instruct the patient on what to work on. Say: "You are going to work on ___" using their EXACT stated words!
5. Verbatim Allergy Script:
Must ask: "Which allergies or reactions to medications have you had? Do you have any severe allergies to other substances?" + Confirm reaction.
6. Verbatim Lead Recommendation:
Must say: "I recommend that I lead, I will provide the drug name, strength, dosage form, and prescriber's name; I will need you to tell me how you take it and why. Or, you may lead if you prefer."
7. Open-Ended Sigs & Indications:
Never say "Once daily?". Ask: "How do you take this?" and "What is this for?". If indication unknown, offer ≥ 2 FDA-approved choices.
8. Off-Sig Coaching & Documentation:
If patient takes med differently than prescribed (e.g. Vit D 50k daily), coach back on track AND document what was actually discussed.
9. Conclude Med List Before "NEXT":
Must ask: "Are there any other prescription or over-the-counter / non-prescription medications you are taking?" (includes PRNs used in last 6 mos).
10. Inactive Med Clarification:
When patient says "I'm not taking that", clarify if doctor discontinued it vs non-adherence (if non-adherent, move to active, counsel, add MTP).

Auditor Finding: "Pt did not state the element 'AVE' and the RPh did not ask the pt to provide it. The address on file is a patient identifier so every element of the address must be provided by the patient."

✅ Action Plan for Next Call: Listen carefully as the patient states their address. If they say "123 Main" without saying "Avenue", prompt: "And is that Main Street, Avenue, or Boulevard?" Ensure Street Number, Direction, Street Type, City, and State are all confirmed.
❌ Verification of Mailing Address MISSED

Auditor Finding: "The full MAILING ADDRESS was not stated. The full mailing address must be stated by either the pt, caregiver, or RPh. RPh can read this full address to the patient because it has already been verified on the previous screen."

✅ Action Plan for Next Call: When confirming where to mail the Medication Action Plan, state: "And we will send your personalized medication list to 123 Main Avenue, Apartment 4B, Detroit, Michigan. Is that the correct mailing address for you?"
❌ Summary & Patient-Directed Goals MISSED

Auditor Finding: "The RPh instructed the patient on what to work on instead of allowing the patient to identify their own goals. RPh says 'You are going to work on ___' (states one thing the pt said during consult)."

✅ Action Plan for Next Call: Ask: "What is one health goal you would like to work on?" Listen to their answer, and at closing say verbatim: "So based on what you shared, you are going to work on [patient's exact words, e.g. checking your blood sugar before breakfast]."

📝 Domain 2: Documentation & Medication Review Rules

❌ Verbatim Allergy Scripting & Pre-Populated Allergens

Auditor Finding: "RPh stated 'What allergies do you have besides codeine?' instead of verbatim script. RPh failed to confirm the reaction to codeine."

✅ Action Plan: Always read verbatim: "Which allergies or reactions to medications have you had? Do you have any severe allergies to other substances?" For pre-populated codeine: "I see codeine listed on your chart—what specific reaction did you have when taking it?"
❌ Leading the Med Review (Offer to Lead)

Auditor Finding: "RPh did not make the recommendation to lead the review and did not ask the patient if they preferred to lead."

✅ Action Plan: Read verbatim: "I recommend that I lead, I will provide the drug name, strength, dosage form, and prescriber's name; I will need you to tell me how you take it and why. Or, you may lead if you prefer."
❌ Review of 4 Components & Current Prescriber

Auditor Finding: "Prescriber was not verified for all prescription meds. The prescriber = the doctor who refills it NOW, even if original was inpatient."

✅ Action Plan: For each drug: State Name, Strength, Form, and Prescriber. Blanket statement allowed: "Does Dr. Smith handle all of these prescriptions for you?"
❌ Patient-Stated Sigs, Off-Sig Coaching & Indications

Auditor Finding: "Pt said taking Vit D 50k once every day. RPh failed to coach back on track and documented 'once a month'. RPh asked close-ended questions for Eliquis and Vit D indication without offering ≥ 2 FDA-approved choices."

✅ Action Plan:
• Sig Ask: "Tell me exactly how you take this pill."
• Off-Sig Coaching: "Vitamin D 50,000 units is a very high strength designed to be taken only once a week (or month). Taking it every day can lead to high calcium toxicity. Let's make sure you take it only once a week."
• Documentation MUST match discussion audio.
• Unknown Indication Ask: "Have you ever been told this is for preventing blood clots / stroke, or for irregular heartbeat?" (Offer ≥ 2 FDA options!).
❌ End of Med List Check & Inactive Med Clarification

Auditor Finding: "RPh did not ask about other Rx or OTCs before clicking NEXT. When patient said 'I am not taking that' for Amitriptyline and Escitalopram, RPh omitted them without clarifying if doctor stopped them vs non-adherence."

✅ Action Plan:
1. Conclude med list: "Are there any other prescription or over-the-counter medications, vitamins, or supplements you are taking?"
2. Clarify inactive meds: "Did your doctor tell you to stop taking Amitriptyline, or did you decide to stop it on your own due to side effects?" If stopped on own → Move to active list, counsel, add MTP.

🩺 Domain 3: Clinical Alerts (High Blood Pressure Alert)

❌ High Blood Pressure Patient Education Alert Protocol

Auditor Finding: "RPh failed to: teach pt 1 item from expanded list. RPh must ask pt if they check BP at home and obtain recent values. RPh selected 'Yes' when they did not teach 1 item."

✅ 3-Step BP Alert Protocol:
1. Confirm: "I see you take medication for high blood pressure—is that correct?"
2. Assess: "Do you check your blood pressure at home, and what was your most recent reading?" (Only ask if doctor was happy with office reading if they do NOT check at home).
3. Teach 1 Thing: "The main goal of your blood pressure pill is to keep your numbers controlled so your heart and kidneys are protected from heart attacks or strokes down the road."
4. Document: Select "Yes" ONLY after teaching 1 item.
