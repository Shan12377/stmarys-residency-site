# What Leadership Values (and how to show it)

A pharmacist hired without residency training is often judged on three questions: Is she trained? Does she know the guidelines? Can she work alone? Answer each with evidence, not words.

| The question | What a resident would show | What you will do |
|---|---|---|
| "No residency" | Structured training across rotations | This 12 week plan, finished and documented. Keep `log.md` current; it is your training record. |
| "Will she know the guidelines?" | Quotes the guideline, year and recommendation | Every module page has the source and year. Drill the "quote it" lines. |
| "Can she work alone on nights?" | Safe, independent verification; knows when to escalate | Codes, trauma and stroke modules come early. Know who to call for each service. |
| "Will she fit in?" | Easy to work with, reliable, asks good questions | Take hard shifts, close the loop on every call, thank the techs. |
| "Will she stay current?" | CE, journal club, safety alerts | ISMP and FDA weekly; one CE event per quarter. |

## What hospital pharmacy leaders are measured on
- Medication errors and adverse drug events (and catching them before they reach the patient).
- Turnaround time for STAT and first doses.
- Joint Commission readiness: high alert drug processes, look alike sound alike, compounding compliance.
- Drug cost and waste; drug shortages.
- Antimicrobial stewardship metrics.

When you say something in a huddle or a call, tie it to one of these.

## Relationships
- After your first weeks, show the pharmacy leaders one module and one case you worked through. Ask one specific question, not a general one.
- A short note at 30 and 90 days on what you completed (modules, interventions, inservice) shows the residency equivalent in writing.

## Verify locally at St. Mary's
- Who covers NICU and PICU verification on nights?
- Is there a pediatric or neonatal clinical pharmacist, and when are they on?
- Where are the order sets, protocols and policies kept (intranet, Lexicomp, Cerner)?
