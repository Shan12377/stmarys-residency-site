## 1. ISMP high alert medications (acute care, 2024 list)
- **2024 change:** tranexamic acid injection was **added** after mix ups with look alike anesthetic vials; given by the neuraxial (spinal) route by mistake, it is a potent neurotoxin with about 50% mortality ✅✅ [1][2]. Store it away from spinal anesthetics. See [[JC_CRASH-2]] and [[JC_WOMAN]].
- **Named high alert in this wiki already:** heparin and other anticoagulants, insulin, IV magnesium sulfate, concentrated potassium chloride and potassium phosphate, oxytocin, opioids, neuromuscular blockers, chemotherapy (vincristine route), hypertonic saline, parenteral nutrition, vasopressors ✅ [1] for oxytocin and potassium phosphate; the rest ⚠️ [VERIFY against the full ISMP PDF].
- **Safeguards that work:** standard concentrations, smart pump libraries, independent double checks where they make sense, premixes, removing concentrated electrolytes from units, tall man lettering, separate storage.

Full list: [ISMP high alert medications in acute care settings](https://home.ecri.org/blogs/ismp-resources/high-alert-medications-in-acute-care-settings) (download from the page).

## 2. Joint Commission: National Performance Goals (from January 1, 2026)
- The **National Patient Safety Goals were retired** and replaced by **14 National Performance Goals** for hospitals; existing requirements were reorganized, not added ✅✅ [3][4].
- **Goal 14, Effectively managing medications,** includes ✅ [3]:
  - Label all medications, containers and solutions, on and off the sterile field.
  - Reduce harm from anticoagulants ([[Anticoagulation_Core]]).
  - Keep and pass on accurate medication information.
  - Pharmacist review of orders (and retrospective review when pharmacy is not open 24/7).
  - Policies for automated dispensing cabinets and override review.
  - Standardize and limit drug concentrations.
  - Shortage alerts and written substitution protocols.
  - An antimicrobial stewardship program ([[ID_Core]]).
- In interviews, saying "National Performance Goals, which replaced the NPSGs in 2026" shows you are current.

## 3. Do Not Use abbreviations and order safety
- "U" for units, "IU", "QD", "QOD", trailing zero (1.0 mg), missing leading zero (.5 mg), "MS", "MSO4", "MgSO4": write "unit," "International Unit," "daily," "every other day," "X mg," "0.X mg" and the full drug name ✅ [Becker's: Joint Commission Do Not Use list](https://beckersasc.com/asc-accreditation-and-patient-safety/the-joint-commission-eliminate-these-5-abbreviations.html).
- Weekly methotrexate, vincristine minibags, and weight in kg only are classic ISMP best practices ([[Cases_Anticoag_and_Verification]]).

## 4. Error reporting and just culture
- Report every error and near miss through St. Mary's system; ISMP's national reporting program takes voluntary reports ✅ [ISMP](https://www.ismp.org/).
- Just culture: console human error, coach at risk behavior, act on reckless behavior. Fix the system first ⚠️ [VERIFY source].
- Root cause analysis for sentinel events.

## 5. Florida law for a hospital pharmacist
| Topic | Rule | Check | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| License renewal | Every 2 years; current licenses expire midnight Eastern, September 30, 2027. Report CE in CE Broker; don't mail certificates | ✅✅ | [5][9] |
| CE total | 30 hours per renewal; any ACPE provider | ✅✅ | [5][6] |
| Required topics | 2 h medication errors and 2 h controlled substances (both Board approved, within the 24 months); 1 h HIV/AIDS at the first renewal | ✅✅ | [5][6][9] |
| Live hours | **No live hour requirement** since the rule was amended effective May 22, 2025 (the old rule required 10 of 30 live). The Board renewal page lists none | ✅✅ | [9][10] (Updated 2026-10-17; course vendor pages still show the old rule) |
| First renewal | License issued **less than 12 months** before the September 30, 2027 expiration: Board page lists 1 h HIV/AIDS, 2 h medication errors, 2 h controlled substances (the rule text itself says no courses besides HIV/AIDS). Issued **12 months or more** before: 15 hours, including those 5 | ✅✅ | [9][10]; follow the Board page and CE Broker |
| **Her first renewal** | License issued November 2025, which is more than 12 months before September 30, 2027: **15 hours**, including 2 h medication errors, 2 h controlled substances and 1 h HIV/AIDS; no live hours required | ✅✅ | [9][10] |
| Immunizations | 3 h every 2 years if certified to vaccinate | ✅✅ | [5][6] |
| Consultant pharmacist | 24 extra hours every 2 years | ✅✅ | [5][6] |
| PDMP (E-FORCSE) | Dispensers report by close of the next business day; institutional pharmacies that **administer** controlled substances to patients can file an exemption | ✅ | [8] |
| Inpatient vs discharge | Discharge prescriptions filled by an outpatient pharmacy follow PDMP checking and reporting rules ⚠️ | ⚠️ | [VERIFY Florida 893.055 and St. Mary's policy] |

**Scope note:** Florida rules change each legislative session; check the Board of Pharmacy before relying on any rule here ([floridaspharmacy.gov](https://floridaspharmacy.gov/)).
