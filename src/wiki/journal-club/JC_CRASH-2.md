# Journal Club: CRASH-2 (TXA in trauma)

**Key:** ✅✅ two independent sources · ✅ one source · ⚠️ [VERIFY] · Template: [[Journal_Club_Template]] · Module: [[Trauma_Core]]

## 0. 60 second bottom line
> "CRASH-2 (Lancet 2010) randomized 20,211 bleeding or at risk adult trauma patients within 8 hours of injury to tranexamic acid 1 g over 10 minutes then 1 g over 8 hours, or placebo. 28 day all cause death was 14.5% vs 16.0% (RR 0.91, 95% CI 0.85 to 0.97; ARR 1.5%, NNT 67). The main limitation is that benefit depends on timing: a later analysis found harm when TXA started more than 3 hours after injury. For our trauma patients, TXA goes in within 3 hours or not at all." ✅✅ [1][2]

## 1. Citation and context
CRASH-2 collaborators. Lancet 2010;376(9734):23-32. 274 hospitals, 40 countries. Before CRASH-2, TXA had surgical data but no large trauma trial. ✅ [1]

## 2. PICO
| | |
|---|---|
| Population | Adults within 8 h of injury with significant bleeding or at risk (SBP below 90 and/or HR above 110, or clinician judgment) ✅ [1] |
| Intervention | TXA 1 g IV over 10 min, then 1 g IV over 8 h ✅✅ [1][2] |
| Comparator | Matched placebo |
| Outcomes | Primary: death in hospital within 4 weeks. Secondary: vascular occlusive events, transfusion, surgery |
| Design | Double blind, placebo controlled, superiority |

## 3. Validity: strengths and red flags
- Strengths: very large, blinded, placebo controlled, simple outcome (death).
- Red flags: cause of death and clot events judged by sites (misclassification likely); transfusion and surgery were similar between groups, so how it works is unclear; mostly low and middle income settings. ✅ [1]

## 4. Results
| Outcome | TXA | Placebo | Effect | ARR / NNT | Check |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| All cause death | 14.5% | 16.0% | RR 0.91 (0.85 to 0.97) | 1.5% / 67 | ✅✅ [1][2] |
| Death due to bleeding | 4.9% | 5.7% | RR 0.85 (0.76 to 0.96) | 0.8% / 125 | ✅✅ [1][2] |
| Any vascular occlusive event | 1.7% | 2.0% | RR 0.84 (0.68 to 1.02), not significant | | ✅ [1] |

**Timing (exploratory, Roberts 2011), death due to bleeding:** within 1 h RR 0.68; 1 to 3 h RR 0.79; after 3 h RR 1.44 (harm). ✅ [1]

## 5. Applicability at St. Mary's
Level I trauma center: TXA belongs in the trauma bay and massive transfusion workflow, with the clock started at injury, not arrival. Ask "Did EMS already give TXA?" to avoid a duplicate load.

## 6. Role in therapy
"For adult trauma patients with significant bleeding, TXA 1 g over 10 minutes then 1 g over 8 hours is reasonable first line if started within 3 hours of injury; after 3 hours it is not recommended."

## 7. Pharmacist's angle
- Check the time of injury before verifying.
- Second gram runs over 8 hours: make sure the infusion is actually ordered.
- Renal adjustment applies to repeated dosing, not the trauma load ⚠️ [VERIFY label].
- Seizure risk rises with high doses ⚠️ [VERIFY].
