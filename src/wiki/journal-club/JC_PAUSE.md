# Journal Club: PAUSE (stopping DOACs around surgery)

**Key:** ✅✅ two independent sources · ✅ one source · ⚠️ [VERIFY] · Template: [[Journal_Club_Template]] · Module: [[Anticoagulation_Core]]

## 0. 60 second bottom line
> "PAUSE (JAMA Internal Medicine 2019) followed 3,007 AF patients on apixaban, dabigatran or rivaroxaban through elective surgery using one simple hold schedule with no bridging and no levels. Thirty day major bleeding was 1.35%, 0.9% and 1.85%, and arterial clots were 0.16%, 0.6% and 0.37%, all within the safety targets. The main limitation is no control group. For our patients, hold 1 day before low bleed risk and 2 days before high bleed risk surgery (longer for dabigatran with CrCl below 50), and never bridge a DOAC." ✅✅ [1][2]

## 1. Citation and context
Douketis JD, et al. JAMA Intern Med 2019;179(11):1469-1478 ✅ [1]. A management cohort, not a randomized trial. 23 sites, enrolled 2014 to 2018.

## 2. The schedule (memorize this)
| Drug | Low bleed risk surgery | High bleed risk surgery | Resume |
|---|---|---|---|
| Apixaban, rivaroxaban (any kidney function); dabigatran with CrCl 50 or more | Skip the day before and the day of | Skip 2 days before, 1 day before and the day of | About 24 h (low) or 48 to 72 h (high) after |
| Dabigatran with CrCl below 50 | Skip 2 days before, 1 day before and the day of | Skip 4, 3, 2 and 1 days before and the day of | Same |
No heparin bridging; prophylactic heparin allowed after surgery for high VTE risk before the DOAC restarts ✅✅ [1][2].

## 3. Validity
- Strengths: large, prospective, real surgeries, standardized protocol, residual drug levels measured.
- Red flags: no control group; 96% white; 30 day follow up; excluded CrCl below 25 (apixaban) or below 30 (dabigatran, rivaroxaban) ✅ [1].

## 4. Results (30 days)
| Outcome | Apixaban (1,257) | Dabigatran (668) | Rivaroxaban (1,082) | Check |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Major bleeding | 1.35% | 0.9% | 1.85% | ✅✅ [1][2] |
| Major bleeding, high bleed risk surgery | 2.96% | 0.88% | 2.95% | ✅ [1] |
| Arterial thromboembolism | 0.16% | 0.6% | 0.37% | ✅ [1] |
| Residual level below 50 ng/mL at surgery | 90.5% | 95.1% | 96.8% | ✅ [1] |

## 5. Applicability and role in therapy
CHEST 2022 adopted this approach. "For AF patients on a DOAC having elective surgery, use the PAUSE schedule without bridging or preoperative levels."

## 6. Pharmacist's angle
- Calculate CrCl for dabigatran; it changes the schedule.
- Epidurals need longer holds (ASRA: 72 h for apixaban and rivaroxaban) than PAUSE ([[Anticoagulation_Core]] section 4).
