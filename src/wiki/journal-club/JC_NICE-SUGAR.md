# Journal Club: NICE-SUGAR (tight glucose control in the ICU)

**Key:** ✅✅ two independent sources · ✅ one source · ⚠️ [VERIFY] · Template: [[Journal_Club_Template]] · Module: [[Adult_Critical_Care]]

## 0. 60 second bottom line
> "NICE-SUGAR (NEJM 2009) randomized 6,104 medical and surgical ICU patients to intensive glucose control (81 to 108 mg/dL) or conventional control (180 or less) with IV insulin. Ninety day death was higher with intensive control, 27.5% vs 24.9% (OR 1.14, 95% CI 1.02 to 1.28; NNH about 38), and severe hypoglycemia was 6.8% vs 0.5%. The main limitation is no blinding. For our ICU patients, target 140 to 180 mg/dL." ✅✅ [1][2]

## 1. Citation and context
NICE-SUGAR Study Investigators. N Engl J Med 2009;360:1283-97 ✅ [ACC: NICE-SUGAR](https://www.acc.org/latest-in-cardiology/clinical-trials/2013/06/09/23/03/nicesugar). 42 centers in Australia, New Zealand, Canada and the US; enrolled 2004 to 2008; publicly funded ✅ [1]. It answered the Leuven trials, which had suggested tight control helped surgical ICU patients.

## 2. PICO
| | |
|---|---|
| Population | Medical and surgical ICU adults expected to stay at least 3 days ✅ [1] |
| Intervention | IV insulin to 81 to 108 mg/dL (4.5 to 6.0 mmol/L) |
| Comparator | Insulin started above 180 mg/dL, stopped below 144, target 180 or less |
| Outcome | Death from any cause at 90 days |
| Design | Randomized, open label, intention to treat ✅ [1] |

## 3. Validity
- Strengths: very large, multinational, patient centered outcome, publicly funded.
- Red flags: not blinded (unavoidable); conventional target differed from Leuven's control arm (180 to 215); subjective "3 days in ICU" entry criterion ✅ [1].

## 4. Results
| Outcome | Intensive | Conventional | Effect | Check |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 90 day death | 27.5% | 24.9% | OR 1.14 (1.02 to 1.28); ARI 2.6%, NNH about 38 | ✅✅ [1][2] |
| Severe hypoglycemia (40 mg/dL or less) | 6.8% | 0.5% | OR 14 (9.0 to 25.9) | ✅✅ [1][2] |
| Subgroups (surgical vs medical, diabetes, sepsis, steroids, APACHE) | | | No significant differences | ✅ [1] |

## 5. Role in therapy
"For critically ill adults, start IV insulin above 180 mg/dL and target 140 to 180 mg/dL (ADA, AACE); tighter targets cause harm." DKA follows its own protocol ([[Insulin_IV_DKA]]).

## 6. Pharmacist's angle
- Reject or modify orders targeting 80 to 110 (simulator case 38).
- Watch hypoglycemia when nutrition stops but insulin keeps running.
