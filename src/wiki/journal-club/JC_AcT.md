# Journal Club: AcT (tenecteplase vs alteplase for stroke)

**Key:** ✅✅ two independent sources · ✅ one source · ⚠️ [VERIFY] · Template: [[Journal_Club_Template]] · Module: [[Stroke_Core]] · Drug: [[Thrombolytics_Stroke]]

## 0. 60 second bottom line
> "AcT (Lancet 2022) randomized 1,600 thrombolysis eligible stroke patients within 4.5 hours at 22 Canadian centers to tenecteplase 0.25 mg/kg (max 25 mg) or alteplase 0.9 mg/kg. Excellent outcome (mRS 0 to 1 at 90 to 120 days) was 36.9% vs 34.8% (difference 2.1%, 95% CI minus 2.6 to 6.9), meeting the minus 5% noninferiority margin. Symptomatic ICH (3.4% vs 3.2%) and death (15.3% vs 15.4%) were similar. The main limitation is open label treatment. For our patients, tenecteplase is a one bolus option with the same results, and the dose must be the stroke dose, not the cardiac dose." ✅✅ [1][2]

## 1. Citation and context
Menon BK, Buck BH, Singh N, et al. Lancet 2022;400(10347):161-169 ✅ [McMaster Optimal Aging: AcT citation](https://www.mcmasteroptimalaging.org/full-article/plus/intravenous-tenecteplase-compared-alteplase-acute-ischaemic-stroke-canada-act-105365). Pragmatic, registry linked.

## 2. PICO
| | |
|---|---|
| Population | Adults 18 or older, ischemic stroke within 4.5 h, eligible for thrombolysis; mean age 74, 48% female ✅ [1] |
| Intervention | Tenecteplase 0.25 mg/kg IV bolus, max 25 mg ✅✅ [1][2] |
| Comparator | Alteplase 0.9 mg/kg, max 90 mg (10% bolus, 90% over 60 min) ✅✅ [1][2] |
| Outcomes | Primary: mRS 0 to 1 at 90 to 120 days. Safety: symptomatic ICH at 24 h, 90 day death |
| Design | Noninferiority, open label, blinded outcome assessment, margin minus 5% ✅✅ [1][2] |

## 3. Validity
- Strengths: real world, large, blinded outcome assessors, both ITT style and safety analyses.
- Red flags: open label; telephone outcome assessment; few primary stroke centers; daytime only enrollment at some sites ✅ [2].
- Noninferiority check: margin stated and met; the whole CI sits above minus 5%.

## 4. Results

## 5. Applicability at St. Mary's
Single bolus helps with transfers and drip and ship. Our biggest risk is the dose: tenecteplase cardiac (STEMI) dosing uses weight bands up to 50 mg. See [[Thrombolytics_Stroke]].

## 6. Role in therapy
"For eligible ischemic stroke within 4.5 hours, tenecteplase 0.25 mg/kg (max 25 mg) is a reasonable first line alternative to alteplase; AHA/ASA 2026 lists it as equally preferred."

## 7. Pharmacist's angle
- Confirm the stroke dose and the max of 25 mg.
- Check the weight band chart and the vial.
- Same BP and antithrombotic rules as alteplase.
