# Journal Club: Tenecteplase after AcT (TRACE-2, ORIGINAL, ATTEST-2)

**Key:** ✅✅ two independent sources · ✅ one source · ⚠️ [VERIFY] · Template: [[Journal_Club_Template]] · First trial: [[JC_AcT]] · Drug: [[Thrombolytics_Stroke]] · Module: [[Stroke_Core]]

## 0. 60 second bottom line
> "Three more randomized, open label trials after AcT compared tenecteplase 0.25 mg/kg (max 25 mg) with alteplase 0.9 mg/kg within 4.5 hours. TRACE-2 (Lancet 2023, China, 1,430 patients), ORIGINAL (JAMA 2024, China, 1,465) and ATTEST-2 (Lancet Neurology 2024, UK, 1,858) all showed tenecteplase was noninferior for 90 day function, with the same symptomatic bleeding (1 to 2%). None showed superiority. Together with AcT, this is why AHA/ASA 2026 lists tenecteplase as equally preferred."

## 2. Validity across the set
- Strengths: four large trials on three continents agree; blinded outcome assessment in most ⚠️ [VERIFY each].
- Red flags: all open label; noninferiority margins differ; two trials in one country; none tested tenecteplase in wake up or extended window stroke.

## 3. Role in therapy
"For eligible ischemic stroke within 4.5 hours, tenecteplase 0.25 mg/kg (max 25 mg) is equally effective and as safe as alteplase, and it's simpler (one bolus)."

## 4. Pharmacist's angle
- Every trial used the **stroke** dose. The STEMI weight band dose (up to 50 mg) is the error to catch (simulator case 7).
- One bolus means no infusion to run out or be stopped early during transfer.
