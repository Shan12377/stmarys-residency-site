# Journal Club: BRIDGE (bridging warfarin around surgery in AF)

**Key:** ✅✅ two independent sources · ✅ one source · ⚠️ [VERIFY] · Template: [[Journal_Club_Template]] · Module: [[Anticoagulation_Core]] · Drug: [[Enoxaparin]]

## 0. 60 second bottom line
> "BRIDGE (NEJM 2015) randomized 1,884 patients with atrial fibrillation on warfarin who stopped it 5 days before elective surgery to dalteparin 100 IU/kg SC twice daily or placebo as a bridge. Arterial clots were 0.4% with no bridge vs 0.3% with bridging, meeting noninferiority. Major bleeding was 1.3% vs 3.2% (p 0.005; NNH about 53 for bridging). The main limitation is that few very high stroke risk patients and no mechanical valves were included. For our AF patients on warfarin, don't bridge." ✅✅ [1][2]

## 1. Citation and context
Douketis JD, Spyropoulos AC, Kaatz S, et al. N Engl J Med 2015;373(9):823-833. NCT00786474. NHLBI funded ✅✅ [1][2]. Before BRIDGE, bridging was common practice with little trial evidence.

## 2. PICO
| | |
|---|---|
| Population | Adults with chronic AF or flutter on warfarin at least 3 months (INR 2 to 3) with at least one CHADS2 risk factor, having elective surgery. Excluded: stroke or TIA in the past 12 weeks, major bleeding in 6 weeks, CrCl below 30, platelets below 100,000, cardiac, intracranial or spinal surgery ✅ [2] |
| Intervention | Dalteparin 100 IU/kg SC BID from 3 days before until about 24 h before surgery; restarted 12 to 24 h (low bleed risk) or 48 to 72 h (high bleed risk) after, until INR 2 or more ✅ [1] |
| Comparator | Matched placebo |
| Outcomes | Efficacy: arterial thromboembolism at 37 days. Safety: major bleeding |
| Design | Randomized, double blind, placebo controlled, noninferiority ✅✅ [1][2] |

## 3. Validity
- Strengths: double blind and placebo controlled (rare for a bridging study); clear protocol.
- Red flags: event rate lower than planned; mean CHADS2 was moderate, so very high risk patients were underrepresented ⚠️ [VERIFY mean CHADS2]; mechanical valves not studied.

## 5. Applicability at St. Mary's
Matches CHEST 2022: strong recommendation against bridging in AF. Mechanical valves and recent clots are a different question; follow cardiology and St. Mary's policy ([[Anticoagulation_Core]]).

## 6. Role in therapy
"For patients with AF on warfarin having elective surgery, stop warfarin 5 days before and don't bridge with LMWH."

## 7. Pharmacist's angle
- Catch reflexive enoxaparin bridge orders for AF ([[Cases_Anticoag_and_Verification]], simulator case 18).
- Restart warfarin the evening of or day after surgery at the usual dose ✅ [1].
