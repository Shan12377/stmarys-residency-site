# Journal Club: NINDS rt-PA Stroke Trial (alteplase within 3 hours)

**Key:** ✅✅ two independent sources · ✅ one source · ⚠️ [VERIFY] · Template: [[Journal_Club_Template]] · Module: [[Stroke_Core]] · Drug: [[Thrombolytics_Stroke]]

## 0. 60 second bottom line
> "The NINDS trial (NEJM 1995) randomized 624 ischemic stroke patients within 3 hours of onset to alteplase 0.9 mg/kg (max 90 mg) or placebo. At 3 months, more alteplase patients had minimal or no disability (Barthel 95 to 100: 50% vs 38%, NNT about 8), with no difference in death. Symptomatic brain bleeding rose from 0.6% to 6.4% (NNH 17). The main limitation is fragility and baseline imbalance. For our stroke patients, this trial is why the dose, the BP limit and the clock all matter." ✅✅ [1][2]

## 1. Citation and context
NINDS rt-PA Stroke Study Group. N Engl J Med 1995;333(24):1581-7. PMID 7477192. The trial that led to FDA approval of alteplase for stroke. ✅✅ [1][3]

## 2. PICO
| | |
|---|---|
| Population | Ischemic stroke, treatment within 3 h of onset (half within 90 min); long bleeding exclusion list ✅ [1] |
| Intervention | Alteplase 0.9 mg/kg, max 90 mg: 10% bolus, 90% over 60 min ✅✅ [2][[Thrombolytics_Stroke]] |
| Comparator | Placebo |
| Outcomes | Part 1: NIHSS improvement of 4 or more at 24 h. Part 2: global test of 4 scales at 3 months |
| Design | Two part, randomized, double blind, 8 US centers (624 total) ✅ [2] |

## 3. Validity
- Strengths: blinded, placebo controlled, patient centered 3 month outcomes.
- Red flags: Part 1 primary was negative (47% vs 39%, not significant) ✅ [2]; baseline imbalance in stroke subtypes; fragility index about 3 ✅ [2] ⚠️ [VERIFY second source].

## 5. Applicability at St. Mary's
Comprehensive stroke center: alteplase is still used, though AHA/ASA 2026 lists tenecteplase as equally preferred (see [[JC_AcT]]). The NINDS exclusions shaped today's eligibility checklist.

## 6. Role in therapy
"For eligible ischemic stroke within the window, alteplase 0.9 mg/kg (max 90 mg) improves 3 month function at the cost of more symptomatic bleeding; tenecteplase is now an equal option."

## 7. Pharmacist's angle
- Weigh the patient; the dose depends on it.
- Waste the excess from the vial before dispensing so the full vial isn't infused.
- BP below 185/110 before, below 180/105 for 24 h after.
- No antithrombotics for 24 h.
