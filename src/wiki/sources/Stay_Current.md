# Stay Current: Sources and Cadence

| Source | What it gives you | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| ISMP Medication Safety Alert! Acute Care (ismp.org) | Real error reports, high alert list, look alike sound alike, targeted best practices | Every issue (biweekly) |
| ISMP List of High Alert Medications in Acute Care | The drugs that need extra checks | Know it cold |
| FDA Drug Safety Communications and MedWatch | Boxed warnings, label changes, recalls | Weekly |
| ASHP Drug Shortages (ashp.org/drug-shortages) | Current shortages and alternatives | Weekly, and before every shift if a drug is short |
| ASHP news and guidelines | Practice standards, statements | Monthly |
| AHA/ASA (stroke), AHA ECC (ACLS, PALS, NRP 2025) | Resuscitation and stroke guidelines | When updated; resuscitation guidelines updated in 2025 |
| ACOG Practice Bulletins and Clinical Practice Guidelines | Obstetrics | When updated |
| AAP (Red Book, clinical reports) | Pediatric infectious disease and neonatal care | When updated |
| IDSA, SCCM (Surviving Sepsis, PADIS) | ID and critical care | When updated |
| Eastern Association for the Surgery of Trauma (EAST), ACS TQIP | Trauma guidelines | When updated |
| Pharmacist's Letter or a podcast (optional) | Quick summaries | Weekly |
| Pyrls (your lifetime access) | Drug summaries, guideline database, calculators | Daily reference; see [[Pyrls_Map]] |

## Inside St. Mary's
- Lexicomp or the hospital's drug information resource (pediatric and neonatal handbooks).
- Hospital order sets in Cerner (Oracle Health PowerChart/PharmNet).
- P&T committee updates and formulary changes.

## Rule
When a guideline updates, drop the PDF or link in `raw/` and ask Claude to update every affected wiki page.
