# 12 Week Plan (PGY1 equivalent, self directed)

Built on the ASHP PGY1 competency areas: R1 Patient Care, R2 Practice Advancement, R3 Leadership, R4 Teaching and Education. Each week: 1 module page, 5 daily cases, 1 "quote it" drill, 1 safety alert.

Order is set by risk at St. Mary's: the specialty areas (NICU, PICU, L&D) and the areas where a PRN pharmacist is alone at night (codes, trauma, stroke, anticoagulation) come first.

## Residency style deliverables (do these and leadership notices)
- **One medication use evaluation (MUE) or quality project in your first 6 months.** Examples: time to TXA in trauma activations, magnesium monitoring compliance on L&D, IV to PO opportunities, vancomycin AUC target attainment. Ask your manager which one helps the department.
- **One inservice** to nurses or pharmacy staff (15 minutes). Example: "Magnesium sulfate toxicity: what the nurse sees first."
- **An intervention log.** Count and categorize what you catch (dose, duplication, interaction, renal adjustment, allergy). This is the number that proves your value.
- **Board certification path (later):** BCPS is the standard credential that signals residency level knowledge. Florida does not require it; it is optional and a long term goal.

## Continuing education priorities
- ASHP Midyear (December) and ASHP Summer Meetings; FSHP (Florida Society of Health System Pharmacists) annual meeting.
- Florida license renewal CE, including the required medication error and controlled substance hours [VERIFY current Florida Board of Pharmacy rule].
- PALS and ACLS provider courses are worth taking even if not required for your role. They give you the code language the teams use.
