## 3. The method (ASHP MUE guideline, 2021, FOCUS PDCA)
1. **Find** the need (high alert drug, past events, new guideline).
2. **Organize** the team (pharmacy, L&D nursing, an obstetrician) and get approval.
3. **Clarify** criteria, objectives, timeline, data collection and how results are shared.
4. **Understand causes** when results miss targets.
5. **Select** the fix, favoring hard wired changes (order set, pump library) over reminders.
6. **Plan,** 7. **Do,** 8. **Check** with follow up data, 9. **Act** to sustain and share.
✅ [1]

**Which drugs make good MUE targets:** high risk or high alert, high cost, high volume, a new safety alert, or not matching the formulary or guidelines ✅ [1].

**Improvement cycles:** the IHI Model for Improvement starts with "What are we trying to accomplish?", then tests changes in small Plan Do Study Act cycles ✅ [2].

## 4. Quality improvement or research?
- **Research** = systematic work meant to produce **generalizable knowledge**, involving human subjects (interaction or identifiable private information). It needs IRB review **before** it starts ✅ [3].
- **QI** for your own hospital usually doesn't need IRB approval, but if you might publish or present it, ask the IRB for a **determination** first ✅ [3].
- Rule of thumb: ask St. Mary's IRB before collecting any data if a poster is the goal ⚠️ [VERIFY Tenet IRB process].
