## <a href="/tools/qbank" target="_blank">▶ Open the question bank</a>

Stepwise cases that walk you to the answer: the questions to ask, why each choice is right or wrong, how a pharmacist thinks, where to find it and who to ask, the SBAR call, and what to monitor. Focus sounds are built in.

**Modules ready:** Hospital Pharmacy 101 (14 cases), Labor & Delivery (40 cases), Vancomycin and Aminoglycoside Dosing (19 cases with full worked math), IV Room and USP 797 (15 cases, your yearly sterile competency), IV to PO Conversions (8 cases), Anticoagulation (16 cases), Infectious Diseases (16 cases), Adult Critical Care (11 cases), Codes and Resuscitation (12 cases), Stroke (10 cases), Trauma (10 cases), NICU (10 cases), PICU (9 cases), Medication Safety and Florida Law (10 cases), Practice Advancement (8 cases), and **Mixed Patients** (8 Top 10% cases that cross modules). 16 modules, 216 cases.

**Levels:** every case is labeled **Foundation** (never miss on day one), **Staff** (solid alone on nights) or **Top 10%** (the catch that makes the team trust you). Filter by level at the top. After 5 cases in a module, the bank suggests moving up (85% or more) or locking in Foundation (below 60%). More are added as each one passes its accuracy audit.
