# IV Compatibility and Administration

Her own quick reference for the IV room and bedside questions: diluent, line, filter, container, light, rate. Built from labels and public guidance. For Y site pairs not listed here, check Trissel's or King Guide through St. Mary's, or [Stabilis](https://www.stabilis.org/) (free).

## Drug by drug
| Drug | Diluent | Line, filter, container | Rate and other rules | Check | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Phenytoin** | Normal saline only (precipitates in dextrose) | In line filter 0.22 to 0.5 micron; flush with saline before and after | Max 50 mg/min in adults (slower in elderly or cardiac patients) with ECG and BP monitoring; finish within 1 h of mixing; purple glove syndrome and extravasation risk | ✅ | [1] ⚠️ second public source |
| **Ceftriaxone** | | Never mixed with calcium solutions; no simultaneous calcium even by different lines (except TPN through a different site when no alternative) | **Contraindicated** in premature neonates up to 41 weeks corrected and in term neonates needing calcium IV; older than 28 days: give sequentially with a flush between, or different lines and sites | ✅ | [2] |
| **Amphotericin B liposomal** | D5W only (dextrose up to D25W) | Flush the line with D5W before and after; filter at least 1 micron (no smaller); protect from light | Over 1 to 2 h; incompatible with saline and TPN | ✅✅ | [3][4] |
| **Nitroglycerin** | D5W or NS; glass bottle | **Non PVC tubing**; avoid in line filters that absorb it | Max 400 mcg/mL; PVC can absorb 40 to 80% of the drug; no blood in the same set | ✅ | [5] |
| **Amiodarone** | D5W | Above 2 mg/mL needs a central line; in line filter; volumetric pump; infusions over 2 h in glass or polyolefin containers | Initial rate no faster than 30 mg/min; leaches DEHP from PVC | ✅ | [6] |
| **Norepinephrine** | D5W or D5NS (label) | Central preferred; peripheral per policy with a large proximal vein | Extravasation: phentolamine | ✅✅ | [[Norepinephrine]] |
| **Vancomycin** | NS or D5W | | About 1 g per hour (longer for bigger doses) to avoid infusion reaction | ✅✅ | [[Vancomycin]] |
| **Potassium chloride** | Premix only | Central above the peripheral limit | Never IV push; 10 mEq/h usual peripheral | ✅✅ | [[Potassium_Chloride_IV]] |
| **Parenteral nutrition** | | Peripheral only if 900 mOsm/L or less ✅ [7]; filters: ASPEN 2020 says 1.2 micron for all PN; INS 2021 says 0.2 micron lipid free and 1.2 micron with lipids; never 0.22 micron on lipids ✅ [8] | Calcium phosphate precipitate killed patients in 1994 (FDA alert) ✅ [8]; pharmacist checks Ca, Phos, Mg stability ✅ [7] | ✅ | [7][8] |
| **Lipid emulsion** | | 1.2 micron filter, not 0.22 | | ✅ | [8] |
| **Vinca alkaloids (vincristine)** | Minibag only, never a syringe | "For intravenous use only. Fatal if given by other routes." | Keep apart from intrathecal doses | ✅✅ | [[Cases_Anticoag_and_Verification]] case 10 |

**Correction to the interview prep notes:** they say PN filters are 0.22 micron for 2 in 1 and 1.2 micron with lipids. That is INS 2021 (and older ASPEN); ASPEN 2020 uses 1.2 micron for all PN. Follow St. Mary's policy ✅ [8].

## Extravasation (ONS/ASCO 2025 for chemo; labels for others)
| Agent | Antidote | Compress | Check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vasopressors (norepinephrine, dopamine) | Phentolamine 5 to 10 mg in 10 to 15 mL NS | | ✅✅ [[Norepinephrine]] |
| Anthracyclines (doxorubicin, daunorubicin, epirubicin, idarubicin) | Dexrazoxane (DMSO if unavailable) | Cold | ✅✅ [9][10] |
| Vinca alkaloids | Hyaluronidase | **Warm** (cold makes it worse) | ✅✅ [9][10] |
| Paclitaxel, docetaxel | Hyaluronidase (conditional) | Warm with hyaluronidase, otherwise cold | ✅ [9] |
| Cisplatin (concentrated, large volume), bendamustine | Sodium thiosulfate | | ✅ [9] |
| Hyperosmolar (dextrose over 10%, calcium, PN) | Hyaluronidase | ⚠️ | ⚠️ [VERIFY] |

**First steps for all:** stop the infusion, leave the catheter in, aspirate, disconnect, give the antidote, compress, elevate, document, and watch the site for days ✅ [9].
