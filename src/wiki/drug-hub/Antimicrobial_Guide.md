# Antimicrobial Guide (empiric regimens by infection, adults)

Her own quick guide, built from public guideline summaries. Normal kidney function doses; adjust for CrCl. Always check St. Mary's antibiogram and order sets first, and narrow once cultures return.
**Key:** ✅✅ two independent sources · ✅ one source · ⚠️ [VERIFY] · Background: [[ID_Core]]

---

## Pneumonia
| Situation | Regimen | Duration | Check |
|---|---|---|---|
| CAP, inpatient, not severe, no MRSA or Pseudomonas risk | Ceftriaxone 1 to 2 g IV daily (or ampicillin sulbactam 1.5 to 3 g q6h) **plus** azithromycin 500 mg daily; **or** levofloxacin 750 mg daily or moxifloxacin 400 mg daily alone | At least 5 days and clinically stable | ✅✅ [1][2] |
| CAP, macrolide and fluoroquinolone both contraindicated | Beta lactam above plus doxycycline 100 mg BID | | ✅✅ [1][2] |
| CAP, severe | Beta lactam plus azithromycin, **or** beta lactam plus levofloxacin or moxifloxacin | | ✅✅ [1][2] |
| CAP with MRSA risk (prior respiratory MRSA, or IV antibiotics and hospitalization in 90 days, locally validated) | Add vancomycin (AUC dosed) or linezolid 600 mg q12h | | ✅✅ [1][2] |
| CAP with Pseudomonas risk | Piperacillin tazobactam 4.5 g q6h, cefepime 2 g q8h, ceftazidime 2 g q8h, meropenem 1 g q8h, imipenem 500 mg q6h, or aztreonam 2 g q8h | | ✅✅ [1][2] |
| HAP, no high risk | One of: piperacillin tazobactam 4.5 g q6h, cefepime 2 g q8h, levofloxacin 750 mg daily, meropenem 1 g q8h | 7 days | ✅✅ [3][4] |
| HAP with Pseudomonas risk or high mortality (shock, ventilator) | Two antipseudomonal agents from different classes (beta lactam plus fluoroquinolone or aminoglycoside) ± MRSA coverage | 7 days | ✅ [3] |
| HAP MRSA coverage | When IV antibiotics in 90 days or local MRSA above 20% (or unknown). Vancomycin or linezolid 600 mg q12h. Stop vancomycin if MRSA nares negative and cultures don't grow MRSA | | ✅ [3][4] |

Steroids are not routine for CAP or influenza pneumonia; they follow the sepsis guideline for refractory shock ✅ [1].

## Urinary tract (2025 IDSA complicated UTI)
| Situation | Regimen | Duration | Check |
|---|---|---|---|
| cUTI or pyelonephritis, no sepsis | Ceftriaxone 2 g IV daily, a fluoroquinolone (avoid if used in past 12 months), or TMP SMX by susceptibility | 7 days (5 to 7 with a fluoroquinolone) | ✅✅ [5][6] |
| Sepsis | Third or fourth generation cephalosporin, piperacillin tazobactam, carbapenem or fluoroquinolone; ertapenem if prior ESBL | 7 days, including bacteremia | ✅✅ [5][6] |
| Don't use for cUTI | Nitrofurantoin, oral fosfomycin (low kidney and blood levels) | | ✅ [5] |
| Oral step down | Once improving and susceptible: levofloxacin, TMP SMX DS BID, cephalexin 1 g TID, amoxicillin clavulanate | | ✅✅ [5][6] |
| Positive culture, no symptoms | Don't treat | | ✅ [6] |

## Skin and soft tissue
| Situation | Regimen | Duration | Check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nonpurulent cellulitis, mild (oral) | Cephalexin 500 mg QID (or 1 g q8h) or cefadroxil | About 5 days (5 to 7) | ✅✅ [7][8] |
| Nonpurulent, moderate to severe (IV) | Cefazolin 2 g IV q8h | 5 to 7 days | ✅ [8] |
| Abscess | **Incision and drainage first.** Antibiotics if over 2 cm or high risk: TMP SMX DS 1 to 2 tabs BID or doxycycline 100 mg BID | 5 days | ✅✅ [7][8] |
| Severe, septic or MRSA | Vancomycin (AUC dosed), linezolid 600 mg q12h, or daptomycin | | ✅ [8] |
| Necrotizing infection | Surgical emergency; broad coverage plus clindamycin for toxin suppression ⚠️ [VERIFY] | | ⚠️ |

## Meningitis (adults)
| Situation | Regimen | Check |
|---|---|---|
| Empiric, age 18 to 50 | Vancomycin 15 to 20 mg/kg q8 to 12h **plus** ceftriaxone 2 g IV q12h | ✅ [9] ⚠️ [VERIFY second source] |
| Age over 50, pregnant or immunocompromised | Add ampicillin 2 g IV q4h (Listeria) | ✅ [9] |
| Severe beta lactam allergy | Replace ceftriaxone with moxifloxacin 400 mg IV daily (keep vancomycin) | ✅ [9] |
| Dexamethasone | 0.15 mg/kg (about 10 mg) IV q6h for 2 to 4 days, started 10 to 20 minutes before or with the first antibiotic; not after antibiotics have started; continue only if pneumococcal | ✅✅ [9][10] |
| Suspected HSV | Acyclovir 10 mg/kg IV q8h | ✅ [9] |
| Neonates and children | See [[NICU_Core]] and [[PICU_Core]] | |

## C. difficile
Fidaxomicin 200 mg PO BID x 10 days, or vancomycin 125 mg PO QID x 10 days ✅✅ (see [[ID_Core]]).

## Surgical prophylaxis
Cefazolin 2 g IV (3 g if 120 kg or more) within 60 minutes before incision ⚠️ [VERIFY ASHP 2013 guideline]. See [[Trauma_Core]] for open fractures.
