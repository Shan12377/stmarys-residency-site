# Pediatric and Neonatal Dosing

Her own quick reference for the floor, ED and NICU questions that come at night. Emergency and code doses live on [[PICU_Core]] (PALS 2025, status epilepticus, RSI) and [[NICU_Core]] (NRP, caffeine, dextrose). For anything not here, use St. Mary's pediatric reference (pediatric dosage handbook or Neofax through St. Mary's) and the order sets.
**Key:** ✅✅ two independent sources · ✅ one source · ⚠️ [VERIFY]

---

## 1. The 6 checks on every pediatric order
1. **Weight in kg,** measured, current, and the same weight everywhere (pounds entered as kg is the classic 2.2 fold error).
2. **mg/kg dose and interval** for this indication.
3. **Never above the adult max** (per dose and per day). Once a child reaches about adult weight, adult dosing applies.
4. **Concentration and volume make sense** for the syringe or pump (decimal errors are 10 fold errors).
5. **Age limits:** neonates (first 28 days) and preterm infants dose by gestational and postnatal age.
6. **Duplicates:** acetaminophen hides in combination products.

## 2. Everyday doses
| Drug | Dose | Max and limits | Check | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Acetaminophen** (oral) | 15 mg/kg every 4 to 6 h | No more than 5 doses a day; adult dosing at adult weight; check the concentration on the label | ✅✅ | [1][2]; daily mg max ⚠️ (75 mg/kg/day is a common cap) |
| **Ibuprofen** | 10 mg/kg every 6 to 8 h | Age 6 months or older; no more than 4 doses a day (40 mg/kg/day); max 600 mg a dose | ✅✅ | [1][2] |
| **Epinephrine IM for anaphylaxis** | 0.01 mg/kg of **1 mg/mL**, thigh | Max 0.5 mg a dose; repeat every 5 to 10 min; never give 1 mg/mL IV | ✅✅ | [3]; matches PALS weight math in [[PICU_Core]] |
| Auto injectors | 0.15 mg for small children; 0.3 mg for about 25 to 30 kg and up | Confirm band with St. Mary's protocol | ✅ | [3] ⚠️ [VERIFY band edges] |
| **Dexamethasone for croup** | 0.6 mg/kg PO once | Max 12 mg in one pathway (others use 16 mg) ⚠️; IM equal to PO | ✅✅ dose | [4][5]; max ⚠️ |
| Racemic epinephrine (croup) | 0.5 mg nebulized | Watch 2 h for rebound | ✅ | [4] |
| **Naloxone, overdose** | 0.1 mg/kg IV/IO, max 2 mg, repeat every 2 to 3 min | Label starts at 0.01 mg/kg, then 0.1 mg/kg if no response | ✅✅ | [6][7] |
| Naloxone, neonate | 0.01 mg/kg IV, IM or SC | Not for routine delivery room use (NRP) ⚠️ | ✅ | [7] |
| **Amoxicillin, otitis media** | 80 to 90 mg/kg/day divided BID | Max 2 g a dose, 4 g a day; 5 to 10 days by age and severity | ✅✅ | [8][9] |
| Amoxicillin clavulanate, otitis (recent amoxicillin, conjunctivitis, failure) | 90 mg/kg/day amoxicillin + 6.4 mg/kg/day clavulanate, BID | Use the ES 600 mg/5 mL product so clavulanate stays low ⚠️ | ✅✅ | [8][9] |
| Cefdinir (non severe penicillin allergy) | 14 mg/kg/day in 1 to 2 doses | Max 600 mg a day | ✅ | [8] |
| Ceftriaxone (otitis) | 50 mg/kg IM/IV once daily | Max 2 g; 1 to 3 days | ✅ | [8] |
| Amoxicillin, strep throat | 50 mg/kg once daily x 10 days | Max 1,000 mg | ✅ | [8] |
| Benzathine penicillin G, strep throat | Under 27 kg: 600,000 units IM; 27 kg and up: 1.2 million units IM | Single dose | ✅ | [8] |
| **Ondansetron** (oral, gastroenteritis) | 0.15 mg/kg once, **max 8 mg**; or by weight: 8 to 15 kg 2 mg, 15 to 30 kg 4 mg, over 30 kg 6 to 8 mg | Age 6 months or older; single dose (repeat once if vomited within 15 min); diarrhea is the main side effect | ✅✅ | [Canadian Paediatric Society: oral ondansetron](https://cps.ca/documents/position/oral-ondansetron); [AAFP: ondansetron in gastroenteritis](https://www.aafp.org/pubs/afp/issues/2006/0901/p830.html) |

**FDA restrictions:** codeine (pain or cough) and tramadol (pain) are contraindicated under age 12, and tramadol is contraindicated under 18 after tonsillectomy or adenoidectomy ✅ [10].

## 3. Neonatal antibiotics (early onset sepsis)
Doses vary by gestational age (GA) and postnatal age (PNA). These are from one children's hospital guideline and one neonatal formulary; **St. Mary's NICU order set wins** ⚠️.

| Drug | Dose | Notes | Check | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Ampicillin** | 50 mg/kg per dose; interval q8h to q12h by GA and PNA | Term, under 7 days: q8h in one guideline, q12h in the other ⚠️ | ✅✅ dose | [11][12] |
| Ampicillin, meningitis | 100 mg/kg per dose (q8h in the first week in one source) | 75 mg/kg q6h is another option [11] | ✅✅ | [11][12] |
| **Gentamicin** | 4 to 5 mg/kg; interval q24h to q48h by GA and PNA | Trough if therapy goes past 36 h | ✅ | [11] · [[Gentamicin]] |
| Duration | Stop at 36 h if blood culture negative and baby is well | | ✅ | [11] |
| Cefepime | Consider instead of gentamicin for suspected meningitis or kidney problems | Dose ⚠️ | ✅ | [11] |
| Ceftriaxone | **Avoid** in neonates with calcium IV or high bilirubin | | ✅ | [[IV_Compatibility]] |
