Tenecteplase and Alteplase (acute ischemic stroke) · Drug Monograph

Drug Monographs section. Built from public sources. Confirm against St. Mary’s stroke alert order set. Key: ✅✅ two independent sources · ✅ one source · ⚠️ [VERIFY]

ISMP High Alert Medication (thrombolytics). Look alike names and packaging: alteplase vs tenecteplase, and stroke dose vs cardiac dose.

Dosing

AgentStroke doseCheckSources
Tenecteplase0.25 mg/kg actual body weight, max 25 mg, single IV bolus over 5 seconds, then NS flush (not dextrose compatible)✅✅[1] SUNY Downstate pharmacy update 2025; [2] AHA/ASA 2026 summary; FDA approval for stroke March 2025 [3]
Tenecteplase cardiac (STEMI) bands, for contrastunder 60 kg 30 mg; 60 to 69 kg 35 mg; 70 to 79 kg 40 mg; 80 to 89 kg 45 mg; 90 kg or more 50 mg✅[1]
Alteplase0.9 mg/kg, max 90 mg: 10% bolus over 1 minute, the rest over 60 minutes✅✅[1]; [4] Stroke module sources

2026 AHA/ASA guideline: tenecteplase is equally preferred to alteplase; window up to 4.5 h, or up to 9 h in selected patients by imaging ✅✅ [2][5].

Key contraindications (bleeding risk)

Prior or current intracranial hemorrhage; ischemic stroke, head trauma or intracranial surgery within 3 months; SAH; GI bleed or GI cancer within 21 days; aortic dissection; endocarditis; platelets below 100,000; INR above 1.7; treatment dose enoxaparin within 24 h; recent DOAC; heparin with aPTT above 40 s ✅ [1]. (Full list: St. Mary’s order set.)

Around the dose

  • BP below 185/110 before; below 180/105 for 24 h after ✅ (see Stroke_Core).
  • No antithrombotics for 24 h after.
  • Angioedema and bleeding management: see Stroke_Core.

Plain English memory guide: Mechanism, body timing, and “why never”

ConceptPlain English pictureClinical rule on shift
How it works (Mechanism)A stroke clot is held together by a tight net of fibrin threads. Both tenecteplase and alteplase bind to fibrin inside the clot and turn plasminogen into plasmin (molecular scissors that cut the fibrin net apart). Why Tenecteplase is a 5 second push: Tenecteplase was bioengineered at 3 spots (T-N-K) so it locks onto fibrin more tightly and resists breakdown (PAI-1), giving it a longer half life that works as a single 5 second IV push instead of a 1 hour drip.In the 2026 AHA/ASA guidelines, tenecteplase (0.25 mg/kg, max 25 mg) is equally preferred to alteplase (0.9 mg/kg, max 90 mg) ✅ [2][5].
How long it takes in the body (Body Timing)• Alteplase plasma half life: About 5 minutes (which is why 90% of the dose must be infused continuously over 60 minutes) ✅ [1].
• Tenecteplase plasma half life: About 20 to 24 minutes (which allows a single 5 second IV push) ✅ [1].
• Fibrinogen depletion window: Lasts up to 24 hours (which is why all blood thinners and aspirin are held for 24 hours after the dose) ✅ [1][4].		Document the exact bolus time and ensure no antithrombotics (no aspirin, heparin, or enoxaparin) are ordered for the next 24 hours until the repeat head CT clears ✅ [1][4].
Why NEVER use the 50 mg STEMI vial without wasting excess, and why NEVER mix Tenecteplase with Dextrose• Stroke max is 25 mg (half the cardiac max of 50 mg): Pushing a 40 or 50 mg cardiac STEMI dose into a stroke patient’s fragile brain vessels causes fatal intracranial hemorrhage ✅ [1].
• Never flush Tenecteplase with Dextrose (D5W): Tenecteplase precipitates (turns cloudy) on contact with dextrose lines; always flush the line with 0.9% Normal Saline (NS) before and after the push ✅ [1].		Draw up only the calculated stroke dose (0.25 mg/kg, max 25 mg) into the syringe, waste the remainder of the vial before handing it to the nurse, and flush with NS only ✅ [1].

Pharmacist checklist

  1. Which agent? Read the vial name twice.
  2. Weight: measured or estimated; stroke dose, not cardiac.
  3. Tenecteplase: draw up only the stroke dose and discard the rest per policy.
  4. Anticoagulant history and last dose time.
  5. Document bolus time.
